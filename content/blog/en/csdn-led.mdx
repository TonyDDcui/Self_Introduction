I believe readers of this article are already preparing for the 15th, 16th, or even higher editions of the Lanqiao Cup. Starting from the 14th edition, the competition board has been upgraded from Guoxin Changtian's v10 to v20. Regardless of the physical appearance, how many generations the competition board is updated to, or how much new material is provided on-site while old material is phased out, the competition consistently provides the microcontroller board's schematic diagram and various chip manuals. In the following articles, I will combine the chip manual and schematic to explain my understanding of the IAP15F2K61S2 and its high-level logic programming.
